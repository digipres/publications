abstract: The World Bank Group Archives’ Digital Preservation work program is documented
  as part of an agile value stream diagram for the World Bank unit to which it belongs.
  The value stream diagram enables documentation of the partnerships and coordination
  necessary to ensure that those born-digital records of World Bank Group staff that
  must be preserved will be appropriately captured and ultimately guided into the
  Digital Preservation platform. This poster will show both the broad context, as
  well as drill into details of the Digital Preservation Program. Takeaways will include
  insights into ways digital preservation programs can leverage value stream diagrams
  to support team strategy and cross-organizational communication, improving digital
  preservation outcomes.
