abstract: Format identification is crucial for digital preservation, yet because of
  its complexities it is inconsistently implemented in our workflows. This paper shares
  findings from the “Registries of Good Practice” project and preservation practices
  collated by the Preservation Registries Special Interest Group. Analysing diverse
  institutional workflows, we demonstrate how preservation goals, institutional capacities,
  and specific preservation stages dictated identification requirements. This paper
  emphasizes the need to connect disparate practices and establish evidence-based
  improvements for format identification.
