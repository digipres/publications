abstract: "The poster will describe the Standard Transfer Metadata File (STMF) which
  is being developed at Archives New Zealand (Archives NZ). It comes in two forms:
  an XML schema definition (XSD) and detailed guidelines aimed at government agencies
  which transfer their born-digital records to Archives NZ. Archives NZ processed
  its first digital transfer in 2015, although they accepted urgent digital transfers
  before then. Since then, we have handled several transfers and learned that the
  variety and inconsistence in transferred metadata form and layout is a repeating
  issue, making the process difficult and slower both for the agencies and for the
  digital preservation team and transfer archivists at Archives NZ. \nSTMF is our
  answer to this, and the poster will present the standard, its structure, its metadata
  fields and the way we envision it helping to better facilitate born-digital transfers
  for agencies and Archives NZ. One of the goals of our poster will be to collect
  feedback from iPRES attendees on our standard proposal, as there are archives in
  many countries where a similar standard is already in place."
