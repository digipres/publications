abstract: 'Digital preservation assessment is often seen as a solitary or institutional
  task - conducted behind closed doors, with limited support or shared reflection.
  The POWRR Peer Assessment Program offered a different model: one rooted in community,
  trust, and collaborative learning. Through a year-long process combining structured
  curriculum, self-assessment, peer mentorship, and cohort discussion, participants
  engaged deeply with core digital stewardship frameworks while building confidence
  and connection. This paper explores how the program created a supportive environment
  for interdisciplinary reflection and action, offering a replicable model for community-centered
  capacity-building in digital preservation.'
