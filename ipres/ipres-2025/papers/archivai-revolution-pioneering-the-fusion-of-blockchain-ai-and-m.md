abstract: How can archivists cope with managing large backlogs with limited resources?
  How will they ensure digital authenticity in an era of deepfakes and AI-generated
  content now and in the future?  To tackle this, we developed ArchivAI, a practical
  framework that combines blockchain verification with AI processing. Our system is
  built on a Flask-based API with MongoDB Atlas, keeping documents and metadata separate
  but linked through unique identifiers. Our approach is unique because AI plays a
  direct role in verification. When the system extracts text, identifies entities,
  generates tags, and creates summaries, these actions are cryptographically verifiable.
  Each document is assigned a unique hash stored on a blockchain, ensuring an audit
  trail that prevents tampering. We moved beyond theory to build a working prototype.
  When a document enters the system, it is securely stored, processed by AI for better
  access, and authenticated using blockchain. Our tests show that this method speeds
  up processing while strengthening authenticity. ArchivAI provides a practical solution
  for archives to maintain trust, improve accessibility, and work more efficiently.
