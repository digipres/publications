abstract: This poster explores collaborative work by the University of Sheffield and
  the Digital Preservation Coalition which explored what good practice looks like
  for the digital transfer process. We use the term “Digital Transfer” to refer to
  the means by which born-digital or digitised content is given to an Archive from
  a donor or depositor (either an individual or an organisation). This is also referred
  to as a “deposit”. A digital transfer can be from an external or internal donor
  to an organisation.
