abstract: This paper examines the role of artificial intelligence (AI) and facial
  recognition technologies in advancing digital preservation through archival curation,
  using the Digital Archives project at Oklahoma State University (OSU) as a central
  case study. As memory institutions digitize and scale their collections, AI provides
  powerful tools for metadata enhancement, improved access, and sustainable preservation
  strategies. OSU’s project deploys InsightFace and custom automation to identify
  individuals in over 60,000 historical photographs, bridging documentation gaps and
  fostering cultural and genealogical discovery. The project’s structured workflow—spanning
  input, processing, storage, and interface development—showcases how AI supports
  not only technical innovation but also inclusive, ethical, and community-centered
  practices. This work contributes to the discourse on responsible digital stewardship
  and affirms AI’s potential to create dynamic, accessible, and ethically sustained
  digital heritage collections.
