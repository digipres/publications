abstract: The knowledge surrounding floppy disk technology spans institutional, community
  and generational domains. However, much of this expertise risks being lost due to
  insufficient documentation and generational shifts. This paper presents the initial
  steps of the Future Nostalgia  project, which aims to identify, preserve, and make
  accessible the diverse body of knowledge related to floppy disks. By bridging institutional
  expertise, active community contributions, and intergenerational knowledge, this
  initiative seeks to ensure the longevity of both the material and intellectual heritage
  associated with floppy disk technology.
