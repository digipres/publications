abstract: Video DVD is a very popular media to disseminate videos, offering a variety
  of challenges that needs to be addressed in order to be properly preserved. This
  paper examines the efforts of the National Library of France to ingest, preserve
  and give access to its entire DVD collection. It shows that among the multiple choices
  the best ones are often oriented by the community.
