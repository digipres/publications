abstract: Tools and services that are both reliable and approachable to the end user
  benefit everyone in digital preservation. From validating digital objects to creating
  a submission information package, the goal is to enable a dependable and streamlined
  process. We at the National Digital Preservation Services in Finland have developed
  tools to help with this, two of which will be covered in this paper. The File Validation
  Service is a stand-alone online file validator that helps in catching errors in
  files as early as possible in the life cycle of digital content. The Pre-Ingest
  Library is a Python library that automates the creation of complex metadata documents
  needed for ingesting content into the services.
