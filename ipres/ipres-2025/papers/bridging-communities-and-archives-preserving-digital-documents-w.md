abstract: The Citizen Archive Platform (CAP) is a web-based tool that simplifies the
  transfer of born-digital data from individuals and communities to archives. The
  CAP ensures long-term preservation by adhering to international standards and extracting
  technical metadata. It empowers individuals, communities and cultural initiatives
  to contribute to cultural heritage preservation and raises awareness of digital
  archiving practices, addressing critical gaps in preserving born-digital cultural
  assets.
