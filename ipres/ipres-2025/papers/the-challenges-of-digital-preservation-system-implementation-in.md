abstract: "Over the last two years, the Art Gallery of New South Wales (The Gallery)
  has undergone significant changes in their digital preservation program. The Gallery
  went from being a member of a consortium project with other cultural institutions
  using a common SIP specification based on PREMIS 3.0, to going it alone and completing
  a procurement process resulting in Preservica as our chosen digital preservation
  system. \nThis paper will outline how the uniquity of the Gallery collection warranted
  the pivot from shared system and storage solution to separate digital preservation
  system and  the challenges of implementation so far."
