abstract: Preserving non-official documents is a challenge for archives. Citizens
  and communities lack the resources to build long-term archives, but their digital
  data, such as photos and emails, will play a key role in historical research. The
  development of an educational program and the Citizen Archive Platform helps individuals
  to preserve and share their digital documents with archives. The platform allows
  users to submit data for future archival use. The paper will explore strategies
  for preserving a city’s diverse history.
